Corporate Update

Decouvertes is now a Registered OEM on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM)

Decouvertes Future Tech is now a Registered OEM on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM), so government buyers can procure its indigenous Vision series drones directly.

  • 29 October 2026
  • 3 min read
  • Decouvertes Newsroom
Announcement

Decouvertes Future Tech is now a Registered OEM on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM). Our indigenous Vision series UAV platforms are now available for direct government procurement, from the team that designs, builds and tests them.

What this means for government buyers

The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is the Government of India’s online platform for public procurement, used by government departments, public sector undertakings and agencies to buy goods and services.

As a registered OEM (original equipment manufacturer), Decouvertes is listed as the maker of its own products. Government buyers can procure our Vision series UAV platforms directly through GeM, from the team that designs, builds and tests them, with no intermediary between the buyer and the manufacturer.

What we bring

Design & integration

Airframes, flight software and ground control are developed in-house in Pune.

Quality management

Every platform is tested before delivery.

Made in India

Indigenous drones built in line with the Atmanirbhar Bharat initiative.

The Vision series

  • Vision Nano: a 250 g nano drone for close-range surveillance and reconnaissance
  • Vision 1.0: a carbon-fibre training quadcopter
  • Vision 2.0: a foldable surveillance quadcopter with GPS-denied navigation
  • Vision 3.0: a VTOL fixed-wing UAV with up to 50 km range
  • Vision 4.0: an X8 coaxial heavy-payload drone carrying up to 5 kg

Not sure which platform fits your requirement? Our guide on how to choose a surveillance drone walks through the key questions.

Next steps

Government buyers can find Decouvertes on GeM, or contact us first for specifications, a demonstration, or help configuring a platform for your requirement. Write to hello@decouvertes.in or call +91 95611 03435.
