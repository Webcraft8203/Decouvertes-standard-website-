The Problem

How can government departments buy drones?

Writing a drone requirement can be hard if you haven't bought one before. A vague specification can bring you the wrong aircraft, even when the process is followed correctly.

The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is the Government of India’s online platform for public procurement. Decouvertes is a Registered OEM on it, which lets buyers deal with us as the manufacturer.

Your organisation’s procurement rules still decide the route and process. Your finance or procurement team should confirm what applies to you.

Who It Affects

Who faces this problem

Government departments

They may be buying a drone for the first time.

Public sector undertakings

Inspection and survey needs have to fit procurement rules.

Police and disaster agencies

Operational teams and buyers have to agree on the requirement.

Identify It

Signs you have this problem

  • You need drones but have no written specification
  • You have received offers that are hard to compare
  • Your organisation prefers or requires indigenous products
  • You want a demonstration before deciding
Choose A Solution

What to look for

  • A manufacturer you can question directly
  • Figures stated for clear conditions
  • A live demonstration
  • Training, spares and support included in the plan
Why It Happens

The main causes

01

Unclear specifications

Requirements may be copied from elsewhere, not written for the mission.

02

Too many intermediaries

Resellers may not be able to answer technical questions.

03

Hard to compare

Figures are not always measured the same way.

Implement It

How to solve it, step by step

  1. 1

    Define

    Write down the mission, area, endurance and payload.

  2. 2

    Consult

    Discuss specifications and arrange a demonstration.

  3. 3

    Procure

    Follow your procurement process, including GeM.

  4. 4

    Train

    Plan operator training and support at the start.

Avoid These

Common mistakes

  • Copying a specification from a different kind of mission
  • Leaving training, spares and support out of the requirement
  • Comparing figures measured under different conditions
  • Not involving the people who will fly the aircraft
Before You Decide

Questions to ask a supplier

  • Are you the manufacturer or a reseller?
  • Under what conditions were these figures measured?
  • Can you demonstrate it on a similar mission?
  • What training, spares and support are included?

Questions

Common questions

Is Decouvertes on GeM?

Yes. Decouvertes Future Tech is a Registered OEM on the Government e-Marketplace.

Can we see a demonstration first?

Yes. Contact us to discuss a demonstration for your requirement.

What should a drone requirement include?

The mission, area, endurance, payload, operating conditions, training and support. Avoid copying figures unrelated to your use.

What is GeM?

The Government e-Marketplace is the Government of India’s online platform for public procurement. Decouvertes Future Tech is a Registered OEM on it.

Who should be involved in writing the requirement?

The people who will fly and use the aircraft, along with technical and procurement staff, so that it reflects both the mission and the rules.
