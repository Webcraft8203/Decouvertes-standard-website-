Custom

No standard drone fits your mission? Get a custom UAV

Off-the-shelf drones are built for common needs, so unusual payloads, ranges and conditions can leave you stuck.

The Problem
The Problem

When do you need a custom drone?

If you keep working around what a drone can't do, the drone may be the wrong one. A custom or adapted UAV starts from your mission, not a catalogue.

Consider custom when your payload, range, endurance or environment rules out standard products, or when you need features a supplier won’t add.

Custom doesn’t always mean starting from zero. Adapting an existing platform is often quicker and lower risk, so ask about that first.

Identify It

Signs you have this problem

  • Your sensor won’t physically fit or connect to available drones
  • No product meets your range and endurance together
  • You keep adding workarounds in the field
  • Your supplier can’t make the changes you need
Choose A Solution

What to look for

  • Honest feasibility advice early on
  • In-house design and prototyping
  • Experience with your kind of payload
  • Clear testing before delivery

Questions

Common questions

How much does a custom drone cost?

It depends on the requirement, so we scope it after understanding your mission. Contact us with rough figures and we’ll discuss next steps.

How long does custom UAV development take?

It depends on how new the design is. Adapting an existing platform is usually faster than a new design.

What should I include in my brief?

Your mission, payload weight and size, range, endurance, environment, timeline and quantity.
