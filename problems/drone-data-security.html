Drone data security: keep sensitive footage under your control

Drone video and flight logs can reveal sensitive sites and routines, so where they go matters.

The Problem
The Problem

Are drones a data security risk?

A drone records where you went and what you saw. For defence, government and critical infrastructure, that information can be as sensitive as the mission itself.

They can be. Risks include video being intercepted over an open link, or apps and services sending footage and flight logs to outside servers.

The practical answer is to ask direct questions before buying: what is encrypted, what leaves the device, and who can update the software.

Identify It

Signs you have this problem

  • You fly over sensitive sites or critical infrastructure
  • You don’t know where your drone’s data is stored
  • The app needs an internet account to operate
  • Your procurement rules ask about data handling
Choose A Solution

What to look for

  • AES-encrypted command and video links
  • Local data storage
  • On-device processing
  • A supplier that can answer data questions directly
Why It Happens

The main causes
