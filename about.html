We design and build India’s own drones

Founded in Pune in 2023, we’re a small engineering team building UAV platforms for defence and industry — airframe, flight software and ground control, all done in-house.

Our Story
Since 2023

An engineering company, not a trading one

A lot of “Made in India” in this industry means importing an airframe and bolting on a local nameplate. We do the opposite — our frames are designed here, our flight-controller firmware is written here, and when something breaks in the field, the person who built it is usually the one who fixes it. It’s slower than importing. It’s also the only way to actually own the technology instead of renting someone else’s.

Capabilities

Technology stack

Advanced tools and indigenous expertise across the full UAV development cycle — airframe to autopilot.

CAD/CAM

Every airframe and mechanism is modelled and toleranced in CAD before a single part is cut — from carbon-composite arms to precision-machined mounts — so what we design is what we build.

FEA & Simulation

We simulate structural loads, vibration and thermal behaviour on screen, catching failure points before first flight rather than on the test range.

Rapid Prototyping

In-house 3D printing and CNC let us iterate an airframe revision in days, not weeks — critical while a platform is still being field-tuned.

Payload Integration

Our modular payload bay has carried EO/IR gimbals, LiDAR scanners, multispectral cameras and comms relays — swapped in the field without re-engineering the airframe.

Drone Systems

Flight-controller firmware, navigation and ground-control software are written in-house, giving us full control over performance, security and future upgrades.

Process

Engineering approach

Five stages, same team end to end — nobody hands this off halfway through.

01

Analysis

Figure out what’s actually needed, and whether it’s feasible within budget and timeline.

02

Design

Model it in CAD and simulate loads and airflow before anything is machined.

03

Prototyping

Print or machine a prototype, then fly it and see what the simulation missed.

04

Validation

Push it past its rated limits on the bench and in the field.

05

Delivery

Build the production run and get it into the customer’s hands.
