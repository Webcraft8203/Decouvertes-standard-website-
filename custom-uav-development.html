How We Work

Our UAV Development Process

Six stages take a project from requirement to a delivered aircraft.

  1. 01

    Requirement analysis

    Define the mission, payload, range, endurance and environment, and check what is feasible.

  2. 02

    Design

    Model the airframe and systems in CAD and simulate before anything is built.

  3. 03

    Prototyping

    Build prototypes with 3D printing and CNC, then revise them quickly.

  4. 04

    Integration

    Bring together the flight controller, navigation, data links and payload.

  5. 05

    Testing

    Test on the bench and in the field, then fix what the trials reveal.

  6. 06

    Delivery

    Hand over the tested aircraft, ready for your team to operate.
