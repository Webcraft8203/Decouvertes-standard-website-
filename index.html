Decouvertes: Indian Drone Manufacturer Building a Secure, Self-Reliant Sky

Decouvertes is an Indian drone manufacturer and defence-technology company. We design, prototype and validate every UAV platform in‑house — carbon-composite airframes, redundant flight controllers, secure data links and ground-control software developed by our own team.

Our goal is to help make India a hub for world-class, high-tech defence manufacturing, in line with the Aatmanirbhar Bharat (self-reliant India) initiative.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Decouvertes, our drones and how to work with us.

Still curious? Talk to us
Can government departments buy Decouvertes drones on GeM?

Yes. Decouvertes Future Tech is a Registered OEM on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM), so government buyers can procure our Vision series UAV platforms directly from the manufacturer. Read the announcement.

Is Decouvertes a drone manufacturer in India?

Yes. Decouvertes Future Tech is an Indian drone manufacturer based in Pune. We design and build our own UAV airframes, flight software and ground control station in-house, for defence, government and industrial users.

Which drone is suitable for surveillance and security?

Vision 2.0 is our surveillance drone: a foldable quadcopter with a stabilised PTZ camera for day and night monitoring, GPS-denied navigation and a 25 km range. For wider areas, the Vision 3.0 VTOL fixed-wing UAV covers up to 50 km.

Do you make drones for survey and mapping?

Yes. The Vision 3.0 VTOL fixed-wing UAV is built for long-range mapping without a runway, and the Vision 4.0 coaxial drone carries up to 5 kg of survey and inspection payload for 50 minutes.

Do you build defence drones?

Yes. Our platforms are built for defence and strategic use, including GPS-denied navigation and Direction of Own Artillery Fire support, and have been through field demonstrations and trials with the Indian Army and defence partners.

Which drone carries a heavy payload?

Vision 4.0 is our heavy-payload drone. It is an X8 coaxial multirotor with eight motors, a 12 kg maximum take-off weight, 5 kg payload, 50 minute flight time and 35 km range.

What does Decouvertes do?

Decouvertes Future Tech designs, develops and manufactures indigenous UAV (drone) platforms, along with the ground-control and companion software that flies them, for defence, surveillance, mapping, inspection and industrial operations.

Where is Decouvertes based?

Decouvertes is based in Pimpri-Chinchwad, Pune, Maharashtra, India. The company was founded in 2023 and does its engineering, prototyping and validation in-house.

Are Decouvertes drones made in India?

Yes. The airframe, autopilot, data links and ground software are developed in India, with locally sourced spares and no foreign firmware.

Which drones does Decouvertes offer?

The Vision series: Vision 1.0, the Vision 2.0 heavy-lift foldable quadcopter, the Vision 3.0 VTOL hybrid UAV and the Vision 4.0 coaxial multi-mission UAV, plus a precision aerial delivery system.

Is Decouvertes recognised by the Government of India?

Yes. Decouvertes Future Tech Private Limited holds a DPIIT Certificate of Recognition under the Startup India initiative.

Does Decouvertes make ground control software?

Yes. The Decouvertes Ground Control Station for Windows handles mission planning, live telemetry and payload control, and the company also offers the AI Vision, T12 Drone and Sandesh apps.

How do I request a demo or quote?

Email hello@decouvertes.in or call +91 95611 03435, or use the contact page. Share your terrain, endurance, payload and communications needs and the engineering team will propose a configuration.
