We design, prototype and validate every UAV platform in‑house — carbon-composite airframes, redundant flight controllers, secure data links and ground-control software developed by our own team.

Our Engineering Approach
Why Decouvertes

Engineered for the mission, not the spec sheet

Four principles that hold whether the sortie is a border patrol or a solar-farm inspection.

Sovereign by design

Airframe, autopilot, links and ground software built in India — locally sourced spares, no foreign firmware.

Field-proven, not claimed

Every number is backed by logged flight data from real deployments across altitude, heat, dust and monsoon.

Serviceable on site

Modular construction and standard tooling — an operator can maintain and reconfigure a platform in the field.

One airframe, many payloads

A quick-swap bay carries EO/IR, LiDAR, multispectral or a comms relay — the fit follows the mission.
