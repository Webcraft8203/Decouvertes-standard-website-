In the News

Vision Nano featured on DD News’ Defence Dynamics

India’s national broadcaster covered our 250-gram micro-drone as an example of indigenous drone technology for defence.

  • 29 October 2026
  • 3 min read
  • Source: DD News
In The News

On 24 October 2026, DD News featured Vision Nano, the smallest drone in our Vision series, on its Defence Dynamics programme. The segment looked at how a compact, Indian-built drone can support surveillance and military reconnaissance.

What DD News reported

Introducing the segment, DD News described Vision Nano as a sign of what Indian drone makers can now deliver:

“Designed for surveillance and military reconnaissance, it highlights the potential of indigenous drone technology in strengthening India’s defence capabilities.”

Like every platform in the Vision series, Vision Nano is designed, built and tested by our team in Pune. At 250 grams it can be carried and launched by a single operator for a quick look over a wall, a ridge or a building, and it is also used for videography and pilot training.

Why it matters

Coverage on the national broadcaster is a milestone for a young company. It also reflects a wider shift: Indian forces and agencies are looking for drones that are designed and supported in India, with no foreign firmware and spares that can be sourced locally. That is the gap we started Decouvertes to fill.

We thank DD News and the Defence Dynamics team for featuring our work.
