Tethered drones explained: persistent overwatch without battery limits

How tethered drones work, why they stay up far longer than free-flying drones, where they are used for perimeter and border surveillance, and their limits.

  • 29 October 2026
  • 4 min read
  • Decouvertes engineering team
Explainer

Battery life is the limit on every free-flying drone. A tethered drone gets around it by taking its power from the ground through a cable, so it can hold an observation position for far longer than a battery allows.

How a tethered drone works

A tethered system has three parts: the drone, a ground unit that supplies power, and a cable that connects them. The cable carries power up to the aircraft and, in most systems, a wired data link back down. A winch on the ground unit pays the cable out and reels it in as the drone climbs and lands.

What a tether gives you

  • Long-duration hover: with continuous power from the ground, flight time is no longer set by the battery.
  • A link that is hard to jam: video and control travel through the cable, not over the air, so they are unaffected by RF jamming.
  • A fixed, elevated viewpoint: like a mast that can be raised in minutes, wherever the vehicle or ground unit can go.
  • Simple operation: the drone holds a fixed position above the ground unit, so there is little to fly.

Where tethered drones are used

  • Perimeter security for camps, bases and critical sites
  • Border posts and checkpoints that need a constant watch
  • Large public events and crowd monitoring
  • Disaster response, as a temporary high viewpoint or communications relay

The limits to plan for

A tethered drone cannot patrol. It stays within the length of its cable above the ground unit, so it is an overwatch tool, not a search tool. It also needs a ground unit and power source on site, and the cable adds weight and is affected by wind. For searching a wide area, a free-flying drone, or a VTOL fixed-wing UAV for long distances, is the better choice.

Time in the airLong, powered from the groundLimited by battery
CoverageOne fixed positionCan move, patrol and search
Data linkWired, resistant to RF jammingWireless
Best forPersistent overwatchSearch, patrol, mapping
