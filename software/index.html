Why It’s Different

Built to stay on your device

This is how we think about all four of these, not just the flagship one.

Indigenous Codebase

We wrote all of it ourselves — the GCS, the T12 app, AI Vision, Sandesh. No foreign SDK sitting between you and your own flight data.

No Data Transfer

Telemetry, footage, messages — they stay on whichever device or ground station recorded them. We didn’t build a cloud backend for any of this, on purpose.

Encrypted Command & Control

The link between ground station and airframe is AES-encrypted. So is Sandesh’s comms channel. Same standard, no exceptions.

GPS-Denied Ready

Our nav stack doesn’t fall apart when GPS does, and the GCS and T12 app both know how to keep flying without it.

Works Offline

None of these need the internet to run. Out at a range or a field site with no signal, that’s not a limitation — it’s how they’re meant to work.

Direct Updates

Updates come from us, directly, to people we’ve actually handed the software to. No app store in the middle.
