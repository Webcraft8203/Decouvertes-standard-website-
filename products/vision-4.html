Vision 4.0 Heavy-Payload Coaxial Drone

An X8 coaxial multirotor for heavy payloads and long-endurance missions, with 12 kg MTOW and 50 minutes flight time.

Vision 4.0
Coaxial X8

Heavy payload, long endurance

Vision 4.0 is an X8 coaxial multirotor with eight motors, giving stable, redundant lift on heavy-payload, long-endurance missions. It runs an open autopilot compatible with PX4 and ArduPilot.
