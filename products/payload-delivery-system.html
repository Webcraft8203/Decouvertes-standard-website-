Precision Aerial Delivery System

A quick-release module for controlled aerial delivery of small stores, evaluated with the Indian Army on our heavy-lift platforms.

Payload System
Field-Evaluated

A modular release mechanism, not a new airframe

Our Precision Aerial Delivery System is a quick-swap payload module — not a separate drone — that mounts to the payload bay of our Vision 2.0 and Vision 4.0 heavy-lift platforms. It gives an operator remote, fail-safe control over the release of small stores such as marker rounds, flares, signal charges and light supply loads.

The system has been demonstrated and field-evaluated with Indian Army units under supervised trial conditions at our test ranges in Maharashtra.

Key Features

Engineered for safety and control

Regulatory Note

Aerial delivery of any payload is subject to DGCA drone regulations and Ministry of Defence trial protocols. This system is demonstrated only in controlled, supervised environments with the relevant authorities — see our Terms and Disclaimer for details.
