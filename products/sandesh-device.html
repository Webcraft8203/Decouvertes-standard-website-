Sandesh Communication Device

Reliable, encrypted communication for teams working where there is no mobile network at all.

Sandesh
Off-Grid Communication

Stay in touch where the network ends

Remote terrain, disaster zones and border areas often have no mobile coverage, and that is exactly where teams most need to reach each other. Sandesh is a field communication device that passes messages directly between units, so nothing depends on a tower or a data connection.

Each device joins an encrypted mesh with the others, and it pairs with the Sandesh app on your phone for day-to-day messaging.
