Vision 2.0
Power. Precision.

Prepared for tomorrow

Vision 2.0 is our production heavy-lift platform — a foldable quadcopter engineered for rugged field deployment, with GPS-denied navigation, electronic-warfare support and a stabilised PTZ imaging system built in from the start.

It is evaluated for Direction of Own Artillery Fire (DOAF) — supporting forward observers in correcting and adjusting friendly artillery fire from the air — alongside general surveillance and reconnaissance roles.
