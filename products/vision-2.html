Vision 2.0
Heavy-Lift Foldable

Heavy-lift, foldable, built for contested areas

Vision 2.0 is a foldable carbon-fibre quadcopter for surveillance, artillery fire direction and training. It flies with GPS-denied navigation and AI autonomy, and carries a stabilised PTZ camera for day and night monitoring.
