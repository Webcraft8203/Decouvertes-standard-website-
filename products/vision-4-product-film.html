About this film

A one-minute film of the Decouvertes Vision 4.0 X8 coaxial multirotor: the platform, its headline figures and what it is used for.

Vision 4.0 uses eight motors in a coaxial layout for stable, redundant lift on heavy-payload, long-endurance missions. It is used for monitoring, remote sensing, survey, inspection and search and rescue.
