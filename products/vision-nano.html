Vision Nano
Nano-Class UAV

Small enough to carry, capable enough to matter

Not every task needs a heavy-lift airframe and a ground crew. Vision Nano brings the Vision series approach to a much smaller aircraft: quick to launch, easy to carry and simple enough for one person to fly.

It’s built for aerial video, close-range surveillance and quick looks at places that are awkward or slow to reach on foot.

Where It Fits

One small drone, plenty of jobs

Use it wherever a quick, low-profile aerial view beats setting up a larger system.

Videography

Aerial footage for events, sites, projects and media work, without hiring a full-size platform.

Surveillance

Low-profile, close-range observation of an area, a perimeter or a movement.

Close-range inspection

A fast look at rooftops, structures and hard-to-reach spots before deciding on a bigger job.

Rapid deployment

Out of the bag and airborne quickly when the situation doesn’t leave time to set up.
