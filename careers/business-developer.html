Business Developer

Defence and government sales cycles run in months, not weeks. If that timeline doesn’t frustrate you, this role might.

Careers
The Actual Job

Relationships, not cold calls

We’ve run field demonstrations and acceptance trials with the Indian Army and other partners, and every one of those started with someone building a relationship long before a purchase order existed. That someone would be you — understanding what a defence unit, a government body or an enterprise client is actually trying to solve, and staying in the conversation through trials, evaluations and the paperwork in between.

You won’t be selling a spec sheet. You’ll be the person who can explain what Vision 2.0 or the GCS actually does, in a room full of people deciding whether to trust an indigenous platform over an imported one.

Who Fits This

More important than a sales background

  • You can sit through a slow procurement process without losing the thread of the relationship
  • Comfortable being the non-engineer in a room full of engineers, and translating between them and a client
  • Defence, aerospace or industrial B2B experience helps, but isn’t a hard requirement
  • You’d rather understand a client’s actual problem than push a fixed pitch
